# Kumbaa

The `kumbaa` tools read, and for curators change, the private family archives the signed-in
person belongs to on [Kumbaa](https://kumbaa.app): each family's tree (people, dates,
places, life stories, parents, partners and children), its photos and albums, and who is a
member. Viewers can read; the family's owner and curators can also add, edit, link and
remove people, caption, tag, upload and delete photos, manage albums and invite relatives.
The server's own instructions describe how to use the tools.

Kumbaa can't hand over ownership of a family archive, delete one, make backups or detect
faces. Those stay on the website.
